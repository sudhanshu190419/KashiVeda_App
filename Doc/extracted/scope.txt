1. Product Scope and Objectives

Kashi Veda is a platform for discovering verified Pandits and booking Hindu ritual services, including Home Puja, Temple Puja and Remote Puja, together with handwritten Kundli services and a configurable Vedic Astrology subscription.

Phase 1 focuses on a reliable booking and fulfilment workflow, transparent itemized Samagri/Prasad pricing, customer payments, Pandit availability, Kundli delivery, astrology subscriptions, and web-based administration.

1.1 Phase 1 objectives

Allow customers to discover verified Pandits and view speciality, offerings, pricing and service availability.

Allow customers to book a Pandit for Home, Temple or Remote Puja with the applicable advance-payment rule.

Allow Pandits to define their own service prices and availability while preventing conflicts with confirmed bookings.

Allow customers to select predefined Samagri packages or build a custom Samagri package from priced items.

Allow optional Prasad to be added to a booking.

Support remote live Puja through an approved third-party live-audio/video provider.

Support handwritten Kundli ordering, full upfront payment, scanned PDF delivery and payout after confirmation.

Support configurable Vedic Astrology subscriptions with daily in-app and weekly WhatsApp content.

Provide Admin and Super Admin controls through a web portal and a Pandit dashboard through both app and web.

4. Customer Experience

4.1 Customer discovery

Customer can browse Pandits before committing to a booking, subject to the agreed authentication rules.

Pandit profile should show name, photo, speciality/offerings, service areas, pricing and current availability state.

An unavailable Pandit remains visible with a clear "Currently Unavailable" state.

Customers should see only Pandits who serve the selected customer location for Home/Temple-related services, based on Pandit onboarding service-area data.

4.2 Booking flow

1. Select Puja/ritual.

2. Select Pandit.

3. Select service mode: Home, Temple or Remote.

4. Select location where applicable.

5. Select date/time from available slots.

6. Select Samagri package or build a custom Samagri package.

7. Optionally add Prasad.

8. Review itemized amount and applicable advance.

9. Pay required amount.

10. Booking becomes automatically confirmed after successful payment.

4.3 Customer post-booking

View booking status, service mode, Pandit, date/time, itemized charges and payment history.

Pay any remaining amount when the applicable milestone occurs.

Reschedule once within the configured reschedule window.

Prepone/postpone according to configured timing and fee rules.

Cancel according to configurable cancellation/refund policy.

Join a Remote Puja live session when the booking is eligible.

Submit a public rating and written review after service completion.

5. Pandit Experience

5.1 Onboarding

Pandit provides identity/profile information, contact details, speciality/ritual offerings, service locations and bank details.

Pandit can define their own price for the services they offer.

Pandit can declare handwritten Kundli capability, price and turnaround information.

Admin verifies/approves the Pandit before the profile becomes bookable.

5.2 Availability and calendar

Pandit can mark themselves generally available/unavailable.

Pandit can set future unavailable periods/dates.

Confirmed booking slots are protected: the system must prevent the Pandit from blocking an already-booked slot.

The customer-facing profile shows "Currently Unavailable" when the Pandit is unavailable rather than removing the profile entirely.

5.3 Pandit booking operations

View upcoming, active and completed bookings.

View customer location and selected service mode.

View selected Samagri/Prasad details.

Start Remote Puja session when applicable.

Mark the Puja/service as completed.

Manage Kundli requests and upload the scanned handwritten Kundli PDF.

7.3 Availability conflict protection

The booking engine must use atomic slot checks to prevent double booking.

A Pandit cannot make a confirmed booking slot unavailable.

Future unavailable periods can be created only for slots that are not already committed, or the system must block conflicting entries.

7.4 Rescheduling

Customer may reschedule a confirmed Puja once only.

Customer cannot change the Pandit while rescheduling.

The original service/Puja selection remains the same.

Price does not change because of the new date, except any separately configured prepone fee.

Exact reschedule timing window is an Admin-configurable setting.

Postpone has no additional charge.

Prepone may have an additional fee determined by the applicable configured rule.

8. Samagri, Prasad and Pricing

8.1 Samagri catalog

Admin maintains the master Samagri item catalog and item prices.

Different Pujas may have different recommended or fixed Samagri packages.

Customer may select a predefined package or create a custom package from the available item catalog.

Custom package total is the sum of selected item prices.

Samagri amount is 100% payable upfront and is itemized in the order/booking amount.

8.2 Prasad

Prasad is optional for each Puja.

Prasad is selected/added as an itemized charge similar to Samagri.

The applicable Prasad amount is included in the booking total.

No separate delivery charge is added under the current scope.

9.2 Refund policy

The platform must support configurable cancellation/refund slabs based on the time remaining before the scheduled service.

The exact slab values are to be entered by the business team before production.

The refund engine must clearly calculate the refundable amount and any non-refundable components such as applicable Samagri, Prasad or third-party charges if the business policy specifies them.

9.3 Pandit payout

Kashi Veda collects the customer payment.

Pandit bank details are collected during onboarding.

Kashi Veda initially intends to pass the full Pandit-earned amount to the Pandit, without an application commission unless the business later changes the policy.

Payout is released only after the applicable service/Kundli completion has been confirmed.

The exact confirmation actor/timing and payout batch frequency must be configured before financial production operations.

10. Kundli Service

10.1 Customer workflow

1. Customer opens Kundli service.

2. Customer enters required birth and profile information such as name, date of birth, time of birth and place of birth.

3. Customer views available Pandits who offer handwritten Kundli service.

4. Customer selects a Pandit and sees the Pandit-specific Kundli price.

5. Customer pays 100% upfront.

6. Pandit completes the handwritten Kundli.

7. Pandit uploads a scanned PDF.

8. Customer is notified and receives the PDF.

9. After the required completion confirmation, the Kundli payout becomes eligible for release to the Pandit.

10.2 Ritual + Kundli

A combined Ritual + Kundli offering is supported as a configurable commercial package. The exact package price and calculation method will be defined by Kashi Veda in the Admin configuration.

11. Vedic Astrology Subscription

Admin can create/configure subscription duration.

Admin can configure subscription price.

Customer can purchase the subscription through the approved payment flow.

Daily astrology content is delivered in-app.

Weekly astrology content is delivered through WhatsApp.

The exact content type (for example daily horoscope/personalized update) is controlled by Kashi Veda and may be administered through the Admin panel.

Subscription expiry must be tracked and access to subscription content must stop or change according to the configured expiry policy.

12. Live Remote Puja

Remote Puja includes a live audio/video session between Pandit and customer through a third-party provider.

The customer sees a Join Live action for an eligible Remote booking.

The Pandit sees a Start Live action for an eligible Remote booking.

Admin configures the customer-facing live-service rate, using a per-minute or duration-based model.

Example: Admin can set a rate equivalent to INR 100 per hour; the system calculates the customer charge from the configured rate and booked session duration.

The actual third-party provider and API must be selected and supplied/approved before integration.

16. Security and Non-Functional Requirements

Role-based authorization must be enforced on the server, not only in UI navigation.

Payment status must be verified by trusted gateway webhooks/server-side checks rather than client-only callbacks.

Booking creation must use transactional/atomic conflict protection to prevent double booking.

Customer and Pandit documents, including identity files and Kundli PDFs, must be stored with access controls and signed/authorized download links where applicable.

Bank details must be treated as sensitive data and exposed only to authorized financial/admin workflows.

Audit logs should be maintained for high-impact Admin actions such as Pandit approval, refund overrides, payout approval and Admin account changes.

OTP/email authentication requests must be rate limited if OTP is enabled.

The system should handle retries and webhook idempotency for payment events.

Application errors and critical backend failures should be observable through Sentry or equivalent monitoring.

The design should support India-oriented time zone/date handling (IST) for bookings and schedules.

18. Acceptance Criteria

Customer can browse verified Pandits and see their own service pricing and availability status.

Unavailable Pandits remain visible as unavailable and cannot receive new bookings for unavailable periods.

Customer can book Home, Temple or Remote Puja where configured.

A successful required advance payment automatically confirms the booking and locks the slot.

A Pandit cannot block a slot that already contains a confirmed booking.

Customer can add Samagri via fixed package or custom item selection and the total is calculated correctly.

Customer can optionally add Prasad and see it itemized in the total.

=== TABLE: ERD • Database • RLS • RPC • Edge Functions • Storage • Realtime • Cron • Queues | Version 1.0 • 05 October 2026 ===
Architecture decision: Phase 1 uses a Supabase-native backend. No Fastify or Drizzle layer is required for the approved scope. Supabase PostgreSQL is the system of record; Supabase Auth, RLS, Storage, Realtime, Edge Functions, Cron, Queues and PostGIS provide the backend platform services. External providers such as Razorpay, WhatsApp and the selected live-video service are integrated through server-side Edge Functions.


=== TABLE: Storage paths and signed URLs are controlled by database authorization rules; customers and Pandits only receive files they are entitled to access. | 3. Phase 1 Business Rules Mapped to the Database ===
Rule | Final Phase 1 behavior
Home Puja | 10% advance at booking; remaining 90% after Puja completion.
Remote Puja | 50% advance at booking; remaining 50% when the Puja starts.
Temple Puja | Advance percentage remains configurable because the latest client discussion did not finalize a specific percentage.
Samagri | 100% paid in advance. Customer can choose a predefined package or build a custom package from priced Samagri items.
Prasad | Optional per Puja; added to booking price as a line item.
Pandit pricing | Pandit sets their own current price for each offered Puja. Booking stores a price snapshot.
Booking confirmation | Customer booking becomes automatically confirmed after required advance payment succeeds; Pandit acceptance is not required.
Pandit availability | Pandit can set future unavailable periods. A Pandit cannot block a slot that is already booked.
Rescheduling | One reschedule per booking. Customer cannot change Pandit or Puja. Postpone has no additional charge; prepone may carry a configured fee.
Kundli | 100% paid upfront. Customer chooses Pandit. Pandit uploads scanned PDF. Customer confirmation makes the order payable/eligible for Pandit payout.
Astrology subscription | Admin-configured plan duration and price. Daily in-app and weekly WhatsApp delivery behavior is configurable/content-driven.
Refunds | Refund slabs are configurable and must be finalized before enabling production cancellation flows.
Live Puja | External live provider; Admin configures customer-facing per-minute rate. Provider cost/price calculation is handled server-side.
Payouts | Kashi Veda receives customer payment and later pays the Pandit after the approved service/Kundli completion condition. Initial platform fee is 0% unless changed by Admin.


=== TABLE: Figure 3. Booking domain relationships. | Booking must be treated as a state machine. State transitions are authorized by PostgreSQL functions/Edge Functions, not by unrestricted client updates. ===
State | Meaning
Draft | Optional pre-check state before payment order creation.
Payment Pending | Required payment has been initiated but not successfully captured.
Confirmed | Required advance payment succeeded; slot is locked.
Upcoming | Confirmed booking before start time; may be used for reminders/operations.
In Progress | Puja has started; remote sessions can be active here.
Completed | Puja/service completed; payout eligibility can be evaluated.
Rescheduled | One approved reschedule has moved the booking to a new slot.
Cancelled | Customer or authorized Admin cancellation recorded.
Disputed | Customer complaint is under Admin review.
Resolved | Dispute/refund/payout decision completed.


=== TABLE: 6. Supabase-Native Backend Primitives | 6.1 PostgreSQL RPC / database functions ===
Function | Purpose
create_or_validate_booking | Validates Pandit/Puja availability, prevents double-booking, calculates server-side totals and creates booking/payment obligation records atomically.
confirm_booking_payment | Idempotently moves a booking into Confirmed after a verified successful payment event.
calculate_booking_balance | Returns server-calculated outstanding amount; client never submits a balance amount as truth.
request_booking_reschedule | Enforces one-time reschedule, Pandit/Puja immutability and configured prepone/postpone rules.
cancel_booking_and_calculate_refund | Applies the active cancellation policy and creates the refund instruction snapshot.
mark_booking_completed | Authorizes completion and transitions payout eligibility.
create_kundli_order | Creates a fully paid Kundli order obligation and locks selected Pandit/price snapshot.
confirm_kundli_and_enable_payout | Marks the Kundli order confirmed and makes its payout eligible.
calculate_pandit_earnings | Calculates payout amount from immutable service/payment snapshots.
get_customer_pandits | Filters approved Pandits by service area, availability and optional specialty/location criteria.

=== TABLE: Spatial indexes should be added for the chosen geography columns if radius-based search is used. | 9. RLS / Authorization Model ===
Role | Baseline database permissions
Customer | Read/write own profile, own addresses, own bookings and line items, own payments/refunds, own Kundli orders/documents, own subscriptions/deliveries, submit own reviews.
Pandit | Read/write own Pandit profile/application fields permitted by workflow, own availability/service areas, assigned bookings, own Kundli work, own media and own payout records. Cannot alter locked booking prices after confirmation.
Admin | Manage Pandit verification, catalog, Samagri, Prasad, policies, subscriptions, operational bookings, reviews, refunds/payout approvals and configuration within assigned permissions.
Super Admin | All Admin capabilities plus Admin account creation/deactivation and privileged configuration/audit access.
